## Outbound Strategy & Deliverability Rules

1. **Keep Emails Under 120 Words:** Busy athletic directors and clinic owners read emails on their phones in between sessions. Long pitches get deleted.
2. **Lead with Relevance, Not a Pitch:** Mention their facility or their athletes before bringing up the product.
3. **Low-Friction Call to Action (CTA):** Never ask for a 30-minute demo on email 1. Ask for permission to send a sample pack or review a 1-page guide.
4. **Compliance & DSHEA:** Always maintain Youngevity distributor compliance:
   - State clearly: *"Coach AJ is an independent Youngevity distributor."*
   - Avoid disease-curing claims (e.g. do not claim to cure arthritis or diabetes); focus on *cellular nourishment*, *electrolyte balance*, and *healthy joint/stamina recovery*.
